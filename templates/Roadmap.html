Roadmap of Institutional Growth and Transformation

A focused Short-Term Implementation Roadmap (2025–2027) — four phases that build governance, financial, academic, research, HR, networking, infrastructure, digital and entrepreneurship capacity within a single 0–2 year horizon.

2025 · Phase 1

Foundation & Governance

Stand up governance and quality-assurance frameworks, finalise financial policies and budgets, and launch the Enabler I entrepreneurship policy and leadership structure across all nine Enablers.

2025–26 · Phase 2

Systems & Capacity Building

Modernise digital and physical infrastructure, roll out faculty development and student-support programmes, and stand up structured IRG, research and pre-incubation pipelines.

2026 · Phase 3

Institution-Wide Rollout

Scale curriculum modernisation, networking and collaboration agreements, and entrepreneurship ideation programmes campus-wide, backed by live ERP, LMS and analytics dashboards.

2027 · Phase 4

Short-Term Consolidation

Consolidate every Enabler's short-term goals into standard operating practice — transparent governance, resilient finances, research momentum, and a self-sustaining startup pipeline.

All four phases sit within the 0–2 year short-term horizon that now governs every Enabler's roadmap.
